We're all a bit fatigued by the AI hype cycle, and the latest round of model releases was enough to get some senior devs and luminaries in the space discouraged on X the other day. But once that subsides, there are a number of theories that make this look like a sigmoid curve rather than exponential growth. There are too many reasons for AI companies to *seem* more successful than they really are. We explore those today.

## Topics Covered

- AI fatigue and the discouragement rippling through senior devs and industry luminaries
- Why the growth curve may be a sigmoid (S-curve) rather than an exponential
- The incentives that push AI companies to appear more successful than they are
- "AI psychosis" — the gap between narrative and reality in the current model race
- The great new hope: what a more grounded, human-first path forward looks like
